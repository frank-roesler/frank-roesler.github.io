Inverse Radon-Transform
-----------------------

The Radon transform is a mathematical mapping that has important applications in medical physics. It describes the raw data measured during an X-Ray or computed tomography (so-called [sinograms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radon_transform#/media/File:Sinogram_-_Two_Square_Indicator_Phantom.svg)).  
The *inverse* Radon transform (IRT for short; sometimes also called *filtered backprojection*) allows to reconstruct the original image from the sinogram and is the key ingredient in obtaining high-quality X-Ray or CT images. A MATLAB function that automatically computes the IRT of an image is openly available on my [GitHub page](https://github.com/frank-roesler/Inverse-Radon-transform).

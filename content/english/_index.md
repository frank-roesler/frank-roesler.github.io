I am currently working as a Mathematician at the [Nürnberger Versicherung](https://www.nuernberger.de/). In the past I have held very different roles, including working as a Data Scientist at the [Fraunhofer IIS](https://www.iis.fraunhofer.de/en.html) in Nuremberg, Germany in the group for supply chain services, where I applied machine learning for image recognition in the context of remote sensing. 

Before that I have been a researcher in applied mathematics at the University of Bern in the group of Prof Christiane Tretter and before that I have been part of the research project COCONUT: [Computational Complexity in Quantum Mechanics](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/885904).  
I have also been involved with the project <strong><em>QUEST</em>: [Quantitative Estimates in Spectral Theory and Their Complexity](https://cardiffquest.wordpress.com) at Cardiff. 

Moreover, I am generally keen to explore possible applications of mathematical and computational sciences in daily life. I am always on the lookout for possible [projects](miscellaneous) in which my expertise could be useful.

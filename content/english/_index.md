I am currently a researcher in applied mathematics at the University of Bern in the group of Prof Christiane Tretter. In the recent past, I have been part of the research project COCONUT: [Computational Complexity in Quantum Mechanics](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/885904).  
I have also been involved with the project <strong><em>QUEST</em>: [Quantitative Estimates in Spectral Theory and Their Complexity](https://cardiffquest.wordpress.com) at Cardiff. 

I am generally interested in the spectral theory of non-selfadjoint operators and the approximation of spectra. Past projects involved pseudospectra of non-normal Schr&ouml;dinger Operators and more general resolvent norm estimates of partial Differential operators. Currently I am working on abstract estimates of computational complexity for spectral and resonance problems.

Moreover, I am generally keen to explore possible applications of mathematical and computational sciences in daily life. I am always on the lookout for possible [projects](miscellaneous) in which my expertise could be useful.
